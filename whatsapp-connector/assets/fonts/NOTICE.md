# Bundled font

`NotoSansArabic-Regular.ttf` / `NotoSansArabic-Bold.ttf` are the Noto
Sans Arabic typeface by Google, used here (via pdfkit's built-in
fontkit engine) to render Arabic-script invoice PDFs with correct
contextual glyph shaping (init/medial/final/isolated forms) and full
Latin+digit coverage for mixed Arabic/English/numeric invoice content.

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 -- free to use,
embed, and redistribute, including in commercial products, with no
royalties. Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org/documents/OFL.txt

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/notofonts.github.io (Noto Sans
Arabic, hinted static TTF build).

No paid font service or API is used anywhere in this project -- both
files are downloaded once and committed here, generation happens
entirely inside this process.
